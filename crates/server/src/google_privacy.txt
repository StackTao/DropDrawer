DropDrawer 隐私说明 / DropDrawer Privacy Notice

Google 登录
DropDrawer 向 Google 申请基本身份和已验证电子邮箱。服务端保存 Google
账号的唯一标识以识别同一用户，并将已验证邮箱用于初始账号名称。
服务端用 Google 身份令牌确认登录后签发 DropDrawer 会话。登录过程不保存
Google 令牌，也不读取 Gmail、联系人或 Google Drive。Google Drive
文件缓存由用户另行配置。

数据使用与删除
DropDrawer 登录数据用于访问同一服务器上的账号和抽屉。服务器保存已登记的
设备、抽屉数据和文件元数据。文件可能在设备间直接传输，或存到用户配置的
存储服务。用户创建外链后，获得链接的人可依链接权限访问分享文件。
可以在应用内删除抽屉内容和撤销分享。如需删除账号及关联数据，请联系
所用服务器的管理员。官方服务可通过 Google 授权界面所列的支持邮箱联系。
删除后，备份可能仍保留数据，直至该备份被轮换删除。

Google sign-in
DropDrawer requests your basic Google identity and verified email address. The
server stores your Google account identifier to recognize your account and uses
the verified email as your initial account name. The server verifies the Google
ID token and issues a DropDrawer session. Sign-in stores no Google tokens and
does not read Gmail, Google Contacts, or Google Drive. Users can configure Google
Drive file caching separately.

Use and deletion
DropDrawer uses sign-in data to give you access to the account and drawer on the
server you selected. That server stores registered devices, drawer data, and file
metadata. Files may travel directly between your devices or be cached in storage
you configure. When you create a share link, people with the link can access the
shared files subject to the link's settings.

You can delete drawer items and revoke share links in the app. For account and
associated data deletion, contact your server administrator. For the official
server, use the support address on the Google consent screen. Deleted data may
persist in backups until those backups are rotated out.
